# QuickCart: Product Assumptions

## 1. What product are you planning to build?

We are planning to build QuickCart, a 10-minute grocery delivery app. It helps users order groceries and daily essentials easily.

## 2. What problem does it solve?

It saves users' time by reducing the need to visit grocery shops. Users can order essential items from home.

## 3. Who are the target users?

Our target users are students, working professionals and busy families. It is useful for anyone who needs groceries quickly.

## 4. What is the main use case?

Users can browse grocery products and add the required items to their cart. They can check the total price and proceed to login.

## 5. What are the core features?

The app includes Landing, Products, Cart and Login pages. Users can add or remove products and view their cart total.

## 6. What assumptions are you making about the users?

We assume users prefer quick delivery and a simple shopping experience. They also like adding products to their cart with just one click.

## 7. What will be included in your MVP?

Our MVP includes product browsing, add-to-cart, remove-from-cart and total price calculation. It also includes login and a simple 10-minute delivery interface.

## 8. What will NOT be included in the MVP?

Real payments, live delivery tracking and actual delivery services will not be included. Our project focuses only on the front-end design, with mock product data.
